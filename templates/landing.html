Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does SkillStack combine ratings across different coding platforms?
SkillStack uses a weighted percentile normalization algorithm that maps ratings from LeetCode, CodeChef, Codeforces, and GeeksforGeeks to a unified 3000-point scale based on global difficulty benchmarks.
How often are coding stats and submission heatmaps updated?
Your stats automatically refresh every 24 hours. You can also trigger an instant manual refresh anytime from your user dashboard.
Can I showcase my GitHub projects alongside my DSA stats?
Yes! You can link your top open-source repositories to feature project cards with live tech stack pills, star counts, and demo links on your public profile.
Is SkillStack free to use for students and job seekers?
Yes, SkillStack is 100% free! You can connect all your platforms, generate shareable links, and participate in leaderboards without any cost.
